* Intentional Shortcuts
Since I'm keeping this to a 2 day effort, there's a need to be aware of some time-sinks that I will most likely side-step first.

The design of my interfaces should be sufficient to add some of these areas incrementally with minimal lift.

- Codebase Organisation will be low-priority:

  Getting lost in OCaml’s module system is something I can't afford. I shall keep everything flat until it works. I can modularise later.

- Visualisation trap:

  We shall do barebones visualisation (some basic log dump).

  We shall NOT do browser-based visualisations or JSON timelines until the v0 part works end to end.

- Async trap:

  Lwt/Async is cool, but adds a large learning curve — it will burn our schedule. We will simulate concurrency deterministically first.

- Testing complexity:

  We will resist property testing or QuickCheck; we will just write hand-driven deterministic cases.
